Heading 1 - Page Title

This is the main title of the page, typically used once.

Heading 2 - Section Title

Used for main sections of content. Helps divide the page into parts.

Heading 3 - Subsection

Used under H2 for subtopics or subcategories.

Heading 4 - Sub-subsection

Common for article divisions or detailed content.

Heading 5 - Additional Info

Can be used for captions, side content, or less prominent titles.

Heading 6 - Smallest Heading

The least important heading, often used for minor notes or metadata.
